ParticiPlan

Founder · by Advay Nathany

ParticiPlan starts from one premise: communities deserve infrastructure built with them, not for them. With NGO partners and local educators, we surveyed families on sanitation, water, mobility, healthcare and livelihood, and turned the answers into costed, climate-resilient proposals ranked by effectiveness rather than ease.

The method is now a web app, so other communities and NGOs can run it on their own.

By the numbers

400+residents surveyed
$1,000+CSR funding raised
2eco-friendly toilets built

Details

  • Wrote a white paper translating survey data into infrastructure proposals, and pitched it to industry leaders for funding.
  • Ran an "AI in Education" seminar for teachers at the partner school.
